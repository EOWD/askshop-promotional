# Home page (/) surface brief

Scope: tapmerce.com home page. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: supplement and nutrition brand teams on Shopify evaluating AI shopping. Action: Book a demo. Proof: a working scripted assistant demo (sample products, labeled), Organic's Best live on web and in its app, real app screenshots. Metrics: slots render only when real numbers exist in src/lib/site.ts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site behaves like the assistant itself. A live, tappable assistant demo is the hero; the page refuses the category default of a headline beside a static product screenshot over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm stone ground (#efeae4) with off-white soft cards (radius 28px containers, 14px controls, full pill for chips). Ink #1d1d1f in three values. One saturated action orange (#ff5a1f) used only on things you can press. Bricolage Grotesque display, Figtree text. One-device-pixel hairlines. Tabular digits for every price and day count.

STORY: Visitors see the assistant answer a real supplement question in seconds, learn it runs on their website first and in their own app second, see that it stays inside approved claims, see Organic's Best running it live, and book a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered two-line headline and one-line subtext over a single Book a demo button; directly beneath, a wide assistant card (website widget) already streaming an answer, with question chips to tap and a cart badge that counts adds.

FORM: Warm consumer app surface (dealt challenger, competitive; my grounded list ranked it below the weekly organizer). Signature interaction: tap a question chip, the assistant types, streams the answer with product cards and a source label, Add to cart bumps the cart. Persistent bottom dock with section nav and Book a demo appears after the hero. Seed key ed728273.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
